FAQ

Things people ask.

Is this therapy?

No — and I want to be very clear about that. Wavelength is not therapy, counselling, or medical advice. It's a friendly, judgment-free conversation with a real human. If you're going through something serious, please reach out to a qualified professional or a helpline in your area.

What can I talk about?

Anything you want. Your day, a thought you can't shake, something fun, something boring, something silly. There's no agenda, no script, and no "right" topic.

Why do I have to pay before booking?

Paying first lets me hold the slot for you and prevents no-shows. Once your payment lands, I confirm the booking and we're set.

Voice call or video?

Whatever feels comfortable. Tell me your preference when you book and I'll set up the call link accordingly.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes — up to 4 hours before your booked time. Just message me and I'll move it. Within 4 hours, the slot is non-refundable, but I'll always try to accommodate genuine emergencies.

Is this confidential?

Completely. I don't record calls, don't share details, and treat everything you say as private.
